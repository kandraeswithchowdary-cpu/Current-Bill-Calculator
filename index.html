How the numbers work

Every appliance uses the same formula. A bill “unit” is one kilowatt-hour: a 1000 W device running for one hour.

units = watts × hours ÷ 1000

Weekly usage is scaled by days ÷ 7, and the units are added up. The slab rates are then applied one band at a time, so your first 100 units cost less per unit than the next 100.
